How This Game Works
1. Top Half:

Read the question aloud. The person holding the phone speaks first or answers the prompt.

2. Bottom Half:

Active Listener Cue. Research shows deep intimacy requires listeners to validate and understand, not offer unsolicited advice.

3. Pass the Device:

Answer, then pass to your partner. The phone serves as the talking piece.

The Social Contract (Agreement)

  • Right to Pass: Anyone may pass on any question with zero explanation.
  • Mutual Confidentiality: What is spoken stays strictly between us.
  • Listen to Understand: Resist fixing, correcting, or arguing.
