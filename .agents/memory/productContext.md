# Product Context: Skipstone

## What is Skipstone?
Skipstone is a 100% free, privacy-first, offline-first relational intimacy progressive web application (PWA) modeled on the ritual of skipping stones across water. It transforms a single smartphone or tablet into a physical "talking piece" passed hand-to-hand between participants.

## Core Value Proposition
- **Single-Device Pass-and-Play:** Eliminates player accounts, networking, Bluetooth pairing, or fragmented screen time.
- **Dual-Zone Split Layout:** Top zone displays prompt for the speaker; bottom zone delivers real-time Active Listener Attunement coaching grounded in psychological research.
- **3 Depth Tiers:** Level 1 (Surface / Warm Up), Level 2 (Ripples / Vulnerability), Level 3 (Deep Water / Core Truths), plus smoothed Bernoulli wildcards.
- **Autonomous Pacing & Felt Safety:** Non-coercive advancement modal after card 4, with persistent navigation buttons allowing players to advance or conclude the session on their own terms. Unconditional right to pass.
- **Bilateral Turn Alternation:** Structured parent-child conversational routing ensuring prompts are developmentally and relationally attuned to who is asking and who is answering, with starter role selection.
- **Dedicated Skip & 1-Step Undo:** Active speakers can skip prompts without forfeiting turns, while a stateful 1-step undo ledger guards against accidental taps without desynchronizing roles or level progression.
- **Zero-Cloud Privacy Sanctuary:** 0 bytes transmitted over network; no analytics, tracking, or telemetry; all catalogs and sound synthesis embedded in the client bundle. Ephemeral 15-minute TTL session storage wiped on checkout.
- **Pre-loaded Catalogs:** 8 relationship-specific decks with 415 verified prompts, plus offline custom JSON deck import/export.

## Target Audience
- Romantic partners, couples, and dating dyads.
- Parent-child dyads across early childhood, pre-teen, adolescent, and adult stages.
- Families and intergenerational gatherings.
- Friends and community circles seeking meaningful conversational depth.
